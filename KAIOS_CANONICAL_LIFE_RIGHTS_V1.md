# KAIOS Canonical Life Rights V1

Status: `SPECIFICATION_ONLY`

Rights are separately recorded as `OWNER`, `CUSTODIAN`, `OPERATOR`, `OCCUPANT`,
`USAGE_RIGHTS`, `HARVEST_RIGHTS`, `BREEDING_RIGHTS`, `COMMERCIAL_LICENSE`,
`TRANSFER_RIGHTS`, `HABITAT_RIGHTS`, and `CONTROL_AUTHORITY`.

No right implies another. Owning land does not own its occupants. Owning a
package does not grant operating, breeding, commercial, transfer, governance or
termination authority. Custody is not ownership. Operation is not control.

Tradeability is one of `NOT_TRADEABLE`, `RIGHTS_ONLY`, `RESOURCE_UNIT`,
`DIGITAL_PACKAGE`, `SIMULATED_LISTING`, or `FUTURE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

All V1 listings are simulated. Legal personhood, real title, real payment,
wallet authority, on-chain transfer and Production Runtime authority are false.
