# KAIOS Canonical Life Economy Binding V1

Status: `SPECIFICATION_ONLY`

Each Life records `economic_value`, `resource_value`, `labor_requirement`,
`maintenance_cost`, `harvest_or_output`, `market_role`, `supply_chain_inputs`,
`supply_chain_outputs`, `civilization_requirement`, `technology_requirement`,
`K11520_eligibility`, `rights_package`, and `value_basis`.

Value requires a documented demand, use, replacement cost, service or resource
basis. Mere existence does not create market value. Unsold output is inventory,
not revenue. Inputs, labor, maintenance, logistics, environmental costs and
rights restrictions remain visible. Company and city Life must also obey the
balanced finance and insolvency specifications when those schemas apply.

V1 K11520 eligibility is fixed to `SIMULATED_K11520_ONLY`. It creates no wallet,
real KGEN, settlement, legal title or automatic transfer.
