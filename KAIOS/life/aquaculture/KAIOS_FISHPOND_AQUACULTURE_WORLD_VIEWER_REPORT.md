# KAIOS Fishpond Aquaculture World Viewer Report

Public route: `https://klineodyssey.github.io/kline-odyssey/world-viewer/aquaculture-v1/`

The dedicated Viewer provides operational views for land, construction, water
source and quality, fish and shrimp populations, feed, oxygen, disease risk,
mortality, harvest, cold storage, transport, market orders, inventory, costs,
revenue, cash flow, enterprise status and the event timeline.

Controls expose only local simulation commands: select land, design, staged
construction, time controls, water operations, stocking, feed, aeration,
simulated health review, harvest, cold storage, delivery, market and bounded
failure/restoration scenarios. Export, import and reset remain local and
non-authoritative.

The official homepage, Full World Viewer building catalog and footer link to
the stable route. The Viewer links back to the official homepage, Full World
Viewer, Ecosystem Runtime and the read-only aquaculture API index.

Accessibility includes semantic regions and headings, labels, live status and
error messages, visible keyboard focus, practical touch targets and
reduced-motion support. Responsive verification covers 360x800, 390x844,
768x1024 and 1440x900 and is recorded in the task Closeout.
