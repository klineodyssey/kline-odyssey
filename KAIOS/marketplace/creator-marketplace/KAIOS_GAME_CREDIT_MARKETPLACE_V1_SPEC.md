# KAIOS Game Credit Marketplace V1

`KAIOS_GAME_CREDIT` is a closed simulation accounting unit. It has no blockchain, private key, external price, KGEN conversion or legal monetary effect.

## Issuance

The starter allocation is fixed or rule-bound, recorded as a balanced transfer from a predeclared allocation account, and included in total supply. Runtime mutation cannot silently increase supply.

## Accounts

Required accounts include player wallet, AI worker wallet, household shared account, marketplace inventory, project escrow, company operating account, supplier account, maintenance reserve and fixed starter-allocation source.

## Ledger

Each transfer has a unique entry ID, debit, credit, amount, reason, contract, simulation time and previous/next hashes. Account totals must equal declared supply. Negative balances and duplicate payroll claims are blocked.

## Resource Separation

Credit may allocate or purchase access. It cannot create food, water, oxygen, electricity, compute, fuel, materials, labor, technology, distance or time. No direct credit-to-joule, credit-to-mass or credit-to-KGEN conversion exists.

## Escrow

Escrow reserves payroll, materials, transport, energy, compute, review and contingency. Accepted work releases approved portions. Rework holds relevant portions. Rejection refunds only the eligible unspent balance after valid costs.
