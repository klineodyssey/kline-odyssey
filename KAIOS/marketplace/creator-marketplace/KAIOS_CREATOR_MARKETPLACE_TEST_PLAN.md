# Creator Marketplace Test Plan

## Starter

Verify one grant, duplicate blocking, one land parcel, finite essentials, finite Game Credit, survival demand and body-specific needs.

## Energy Ontology

Verify kinetic and gravitational references in the KAIOS operational layer, the mass-energy reference in the KGEN cosmic layer, and the absence of currency-energy, token-mass and KAIOS-KGEN conversion behavior.

## Marketplace

Verify request decisions, clarification, project escrow, dependencies, candidate dispatch status, listing review, customer acceptance, rework, rejection, delivery and payroll duplicate prevention.

## Economy

Verify balanced ledger, fixed supply, purchases, wallet/inventory deltas, worker-owned wages, supplier payment, maintenance reserve, unsold inventory and no revenue before sale.

## Causality

Verify shelter construction cannot skip land, resources, labor, tools, transport, time or inspection. Verify replay and import/export produce identical state hashes.

## Repository Gates

Run JSON, links, UTF-8, BOM, corruption, secret, protected-path and `git diff --check` checks plus all World Viewer regressions. Required unresolved findings: `P0=0`, `P1=0`, `P2=0`.
