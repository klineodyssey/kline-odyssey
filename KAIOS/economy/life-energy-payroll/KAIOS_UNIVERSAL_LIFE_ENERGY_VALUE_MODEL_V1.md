# KAIOS Universal Life Energy and Value Model V1

Status: `APPROVED_SPECIFICATION`

## Four Layers

1. `KGEN`: real blockchain token; disabled and inaccessible in this runtime.
2. `KAIOS_CREDIT`: universal simulation value and accounting unit for budgets,
   work, costs, services and allocation.
3. `COLONY_OR_GROUP_CREDIT`: internal contribution, ration or allocation
   evidence; not automatically convertible and not a token.
4. `PHYSICAL_OR_ECOLOGICAL_RESOURCE`: finite food, water, oxygen, nectar,
   honey, electricity, compute, fuel, material, shelter and time inventories.

## Conservation Rule

KAIOS Credit may measure or purchase authorized access to a resource. It cannot
create mass, energy, food, water, oxygen, electricity, compute, fuel, material,
labor availability, transport capacity or elapsed time.

Each resource event records a source pool, destination or consumption sink,
quantity, unit, time and causal reference. A value transfer and a resource
transfer are separate events even when one funds the other.

## Needs

- Plants require sunlight, water, soil, nutrients, carbon-dioxide proxy,
  compatible temperature and time.
- Fish require water, oxygen, food, temperature, quality and space.
- Digital AI requires electricity, compute, storage, network, cooling,
  maintenance and security.
- Robotic AI requires electricity or fuel, body integrity, sensors, parts,
  lubrication and repair.

Balances cannot substitute for any missing need. Shortage changes health,
integrity, availability or service state through the owning runtime.

## Issuance Boundary

This workline has no issuance command. Initial scenario balances are explicit
fixtures with a declared source account. All later entries are balanced
transfers, reservation movements, releases or refunds. `KGEN`, chain addresses,
private keys and real settlement are forbidden.
