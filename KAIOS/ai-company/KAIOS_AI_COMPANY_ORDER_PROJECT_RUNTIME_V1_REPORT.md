visible assumptions, sixteen feasibility gates, nine project templates,
dependency graphs, resource plans, schedules, simulation-only contracts,
procurement, task execution, inspection, rework, change orders, delivery,

Economy authority, CURRENT, Constitution sources, Wallet, KGEN or Production
authority.
