## Genome

The versioned genome includes:

- stable genome and parent identifiers
- species and generation
- deterministic random seed
- dominant and recessive allele groups
- mutation and inheritance probabilities
- phenotype projection
- health, metabolism, locomotion, sensory, cognition, aggression, social,
  reproduction, adaptation, lifespan, size, coloration, and morphology traits
- mutation history
- integrity checksum

Equal parent genomes and equal seeds produce equal offspring genomes.
Recombination deterministically chooses inherited values and applies bounded
software-only mutations. The checksum is calculated from the canonical stable
representation and is verified before runtime creation.
