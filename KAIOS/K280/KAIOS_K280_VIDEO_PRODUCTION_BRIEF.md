# K280 Video Production Brief

## Main Title

《K280 寒武紀大爆發：KAIOS如何誕生第一隻數位恐龍》

Production gate: do not publish until the MVP is merged and its public Viewer
and API URLs have passed deployment verification.

Required disclaimer:

> KAIOS-RAPTOR-K280-001 is a digital-life simulation and software organism.
> It is not a real biological dinosaur and does not represent real-world
> cloning, resurrection or genetic engineering.

## Twenty-Two Chapters

1. K280生命實驗場: enter the bounded simulation habitat.
2. KAIOS生命作業系統: show specification, validation, release, and runtime.
3. 物種規格如何建立: inspect taxonomy and the Species manifest.
4. Genome如何生成: visualize deterministic software alleles.
5. 第一隻恐龍如何出生: run the ten-stage local birth pipeline.
6. 生命ID與出生紀錄: separate life, organism, genome, embodiment, and event IDs.
7. 細胞、器官、能量與健康: inspect simulation subsystems.
8. 行為、記憶與性格: follow tick-driven behavior and bounded memory.
9. 成長、繁殖與突變: show maturity gates and inherited variation.
10. K280寒武紀生命大爆發: replay five bounded generations.
11. 物種如何分支: mark variation as a candidate branch, not a new Species.
12. 文明如何從原始採集進入農業: show threshold-based advancement.
13. 工業、資訊、AI與太空文明: show ordered transitions with no skipping.
14. 星際文明: visualize mature space capability.
15. 神仙文明: explain continuity, longevity, energy control, and coordination.
16. 神明文明: explain project-world computation and environmental engineering.
17. 神軍文明: explain governed defense and resilient coordination without
    promoting real-world violence.
18. 恐龍如何封裝成數位生命商品: separate organism state from package metadata.
19. K11520如何分離所有權與使用權: display all ten independent right classes.
20. 模擬上架與虛擬結算: emphasize no wallet, token, or real settlement.
21. 數位生命與真實生物的界線: display the full disclaimer.
22. 未來客戶如何訂製自己的恐龍: demonstrate the safe request workflow.

## 8-12 Minute Main Script

**Act 1: Specification and identity, 0:00-2:30**

Open on the K280 habitat and state the disclaimer. Explain KAIOS as the
operating environment for this software organism. Walk through the canonical
Schema V2 manifest, the 12 taxonomy levels, 19-layer compatibility, Species
program binding, and the separation of life, organism, genome, embodiment,
birth-event, and runtime-instance IDs. Show that all IDs are scoped to the MVP.

**Act 2: Genome and birth, 2:30-4:20**

Generate the founder Genome twice with the same seed and compare checksums.
Change the seed and show a different phenotype. Run the birth pipeline one
stage at a time. Explain that validation is fail-closed and that `RELEASE`
targets only `LOCAL_DETERMINISTIC_SIMULATION`.

**Act 3: Living state, 4:20-6:10**

Start the Viewer. Observe movement, perception, energy use, hunger, hydration,
temperature, behavior, memory, growth, injury, and healing. Pause, serialize,
resume, and replay. Explain the bounded memory and event logs.

**Act 4: Reproduction and diversification, 6:10-7:45**

Show maturity and capacity gates. Recombine parent genomes, apply deterministic
mutation, and run generations 1 through 5. Compare size, coloration, energy
efficiency, senses, social traits, survival, births, deaths, and habitat
pressure. Explain why a branch remains a candidate.

**Act 5: Civilization, 7:45-9:15**

Keep organism and civilization ownership separate. Advance in exact order from
foraging to agriculture, urban, industrial, electrical, information, AI,
spacefaring, interstellar, `IMMORTAL_CIVILIZATION`, `DEITY_CIVILIZATION`, and
`DIVINE_ARMY_CIVILIZATION`. Demonstrate a failed skip and a stability
regression.

**Act 6: Rights and customer workflow, 9:15-10:45**

Open the simulated K11520 card and inspect all rights separately. Explain that
there is no wallet or settlement. Finish with the custom dinosaur request:
requirements, Species match, Genome, package, simulation test, rights package,
preview, and simulated listing.

**Close, 10:45-11:15**

Repeat the disclaimer. End on: 「KAIOS建立的是一個可驗證的數位生命軟體
系統，不是真實生物復活。」 Display the public Viewer and API links only after
deployment verification.

## Narration and Subtitle Style

Narration should use direct, technical Traditional Chinese with short English
codes on screen. Subtitles should be two lines maximum, use the repository's
canonical stage names, and avoid financial promises, real sentience claims,
or biological engineering language.

Subtitle draft anchors:

- 「K280是一個數位生命模擬，不是真實恐龍。」
- 「固定輸入與固定種子，產生可重播結果。」
- 「出生只釋放到本地決定性模擬。」
- 「繁殖有成熟、健康、能量與人口容量限制。」
- 「文明按順序演進，不能跳級。」
