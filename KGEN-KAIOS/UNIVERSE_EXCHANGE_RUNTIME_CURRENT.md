## 2. Role of 11520

11520 is not merely a token exchange. It is the shared admission, review and trading gateway for living civilizations.

```text
11520
=
Asset Market
+ Life Review
+ Species Registry
+ Player-Universe Admission
+ Civilization Listing
+ Liquidity Interface
+ Cross-Universe Settlement Gateway
```

11520 must separate ordinary trading from the legal and biological status of a civilization.

```text
Buying a token
≠ becoming a resident
≠ receiving land
≠ gaining governance rights
≠ passing life review
```

## 4. Application Types

### 4.1 Asset Listing

For a token, commodity, digital good or service that does not claim to be a complete life or civilization.

### 4.2 Life Listing

For an AI, App, robot, company organism or other registered software life.

### 4.3 Species Registration

For a reproducible or evolvable organism with a declared taxonomy and ecological role.

### 4.4 Civilization Listing

For a community with its own rules, work system, economy, culture and resident admission.

### 4.5 Player-Universe Genesis

For a creator seeking a Universe ID, coordinate or land allocation, genesis-mass proof and permission to activate a new ecosystem.

## 6. Genesis Mass

KGEN is the designated genesis-mass asset for player-universe creation unless the canonical physics constitution is later amended.

KAIOS is the public civilization currency and settlement asset. KAIOS does not automatically create a genesis black hole or new Universe ID.

```text
KGEN
= genesis-mass commitment

KAIOS
= civilization money, settlement, work income and service gas

Player token
= local civilization currency
```

### 6.1 Minimum Mass

This version does not invent a universal numeric minimum. The minimum must be defined by civilization class after physics reconciliation.

The system may distinguish:

- soul-mass particle;
- life seed;
- App or workshop organism;
- micro-civilization;
- habitat or asteroid civilization;
- planetary civilization;
- stellar or multi-world civilization.

`10^-18 KGEN` may be technically representable, but it must not automatically qualify as a complete black hole, civilization or universe.

### 6.2 Mass Conservation

A player-universe commitment must be traceable to real KGEN and cannot duplicate the same KGEN across multiple active universes or timelines.

```text
Sum of active genesis-mass commitments
≤ canonical KGEN supply and valid commitments
```

## 10. Liquidity and Pricing

Any lawful asset may be exchanged when a valid market, liquidity pool or order book exists.

Possible pairs include:

```text
KGEN / WBNB
KGEN / USDT
KAIOS / KGEN
KAIOS / WBNB
KAIOS / USDT
Player Token / KGEN
Player Token / KAIOS
Player Token / WBNB
Player Token / USDT
```

11520 must not falsely claim that every pair has liquidity or guaranteed redemption.

### 10.1 Initial Price

For an AMM pool, the creator’s initial deposits establish the opening ratio.

```text
100 KGEN + 1,000,000 ABC
→ initial displayed ratio: 1 KGEN = 10,000 ABC
```

This is an initial market quote, not a permanent guarantee, reserve promise or official valuation.

### 10.2 KGEN–KAIOS White-Hole Ratio

The white-hole rule:

```text
1 KGEN actually destroyed and observed by Friction Mirror
→ 1,000 KAIOS settled to 18888
```

is a one-way genesis conversion ratio, not a guaranteed two-way market price.

KAIOS may trade back into KGEN only through market liquidity at the prevailing price.

### 10.3 Listing Requirement

A civilization may be required to maintain at least one disclosed principal pool, such as:

```text
Player Token / KGEN
or
Player Token / KAIOS
```

Other pools are optional. The chosen pool must not be described as guaranteed liquidity unless reserves and redemption terms actually support that claim.

## 12. External Traders and Residents

A person outside KAIOS may still hold and trade public assets if the blockchain and market allow it.

External traders may:

- buy and sell tokens;
- transfer tokens;
- provide liquidity;
- inspect contracts and prices.

Formal residents may additionally apply for:

- Life ID;
- work status;
- civilization membership;
- land or housing rights;
- species registration;
- public services;
- governance and appeal rights;
- inter-civilization migration.

Market access and civilization rights are deliberately separate.

## 14. Security and Conservation Review

Approval must reject or remediate:

- arbitrary hidden minting;
- owner-only asset seizure;
- fake burn proofs;
- duplicate genesis-mass use;
- unrestricted upgrade backdoors;
- manipulated oracle dependence;
- misleading fixed-price or guaranteed-yield claims;
- new-money-to-old-investor repayment structures;
- malware, fraud or unauthorized surveillance;
- uncontrolled replication or ecological invasion;
- asset duplication across parallel timelines.

A simulation fork may copy state for testing, but only one settlement timeline may redeem the original real assets.

---

## 15. Rights and Governance

11520 review must be transparent and appealable.

The reviewer must distinguish:

- code correctness;
- security risk;
- physics or conservation consistency;
- market disclosure;
- life and resident rights;
- cultural content.

A civilization must not be rejected solely because it does not use Jade Emperor, Yama or Chinese cultural interfaces.
