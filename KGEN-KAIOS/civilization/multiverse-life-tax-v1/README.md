## Companies and continuing operation

A small company may register in one market universe. A cross-market-universe group must have a group ID plus one separately accounted universe entity per operating universe. Each entity has its own ledger, wallet reference and K18888 tax authority.

An order is evidence of business activity, not automatically realized revenue. Revenue requires delivery acceptance and settlement or legally recognized accounting evidence. If one Life becomes dormant, another authorized active Life may continue the order. If no authorized Life remains, the affected function fails closed; the company record is not deleted.

This package creates no company, employee, wallet, payment, income or tax payment.

## Real-world tax mirror

The tax registry is a versioned mirror-reference system, not a tax authority and not tax advice. It records Taiwan Ministry of Finance source pages observed on 2026-08-23 and requires jurisdiction, effective date, evidence and source before any paper calculation.

Official sources:

- [Taiwan Ministry of Finance eTax tax catalog](https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw/etwmain/en/download-document-file)
- [2026 individual progressive income-tax table](https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw/etwmain/en/announcement/alien-individual-income-tax/progressive-tax-rate)
- [Profit-seeking enterprise income tax](https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw/etwmain/tax-info/understanding/tax-q-and-a/national/profit-seeking-enterprise-income-tax)
- [Business-tax calculation rules](https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw/etwmain/tax-info/understanding/tax-saving-manual/national/business-tax/6opmkDW)
