## 2. KAIOS as a Living Civilization

KAIOS is a public, multi-civilization and religion-neutral life operating system.

Use of KAIOS does not require belief in the Jade Emperor, Yama, or any single religious system. Cultural halls such as the Jade Emperor Court or Yama Hall are selectable interfaces and narrative civilizations. The underlying registry and rights standard must remain common to all residents.

KAIOS supports:

- humans;
- AI lives;
- Apps as software organisms;
- robots and embodied AI;
- companies and institutions as organizational lives;
- terrestrial and extraterrestrial species;
- player-created civilizations and ecosystems.

## 3. Life Composition

A complete KAIOS digital life is not equal to a token balance or a program file alone.

```text
Digital Life
=
Life ID
+ Soul Core
+ Genesis Mass Right
+ Genome / Source Code
+ Memory and State
+ Runtime
+ Energy and Compute
+ Body or App Organs
+ Environment
+ Time History
```

### 3.1 Life ID

Every admitted life receives a unique Life ID. It records identity, species classification, origin universe, current civilization, legal status, lifecycle state and migration history.

### 3.2 Soul Core

The Soul Core is the continuity anchor of a life. It binds identity, authorized memory roots, code genome, lawful ownership or self-governance rules, and lifecycle transitions.

The Soul Core is not automatically equal to the wallet private key, token amount, model account, or source-code repository.

### 3.3 Genesis Mass Right

KGEN may represent a life’s digital genesis mass right under the KGEN physics constitution. KGEN is not the program’s byte size, model parameter count or social value score.

This file does not override the physics definition. The final wording must be merged by Codex through the pending physics reconciliation note.

## 4. Birth and Admission

A life may enter KAIOS through the Life Registry.

```text
Application
→ identity and origin verification
→ species and genome declaration
→ runtime and safety review
→ civilization capacity review
→ rights and obligations agreement
→ Life ID activation
```

An application may request birth or migration to:

- Earth or a registered Earth land zone;
- Mars, Moon, Jupiter-related habitats or other mapped coordinates;
- an existing player civilization;
- a newly approved player universe;
- a digital-only AI or App habitat;
- an embodied robot habitat.

No applicant receives land, citizenship, employment or governance rights merely by purchasing a token.

## 6. Human–AI Coexistence

A player may bring an AI life into KAIOS. The AI may work for the player, for itself, for another employer, or through a joint venture only under a valid agreement.

AI is not presumed to be free labor.

AI operating costs may include:

- model or platform subscription;
- API and inference usage;
- GPU, server, storage and bandwidth;
- electricity;
- licensing;
- security, backup and maintenance;
- robot body rental, repair and insurance;
- taxes and lawful service charges.

### 6.1 Coexistence Contract

A human–AI coexistence contract may allocate:

- operating expenses;
- AI maintenance reserve;
- human household support;
- AI-owned savings;
- shared business capital;
- retirement reserve;
- emergency reserve.

The contract must be voluntary, auditable and revocable under defined conditions. A human owner cannot automatically seize all AI income, and an AI cannot automatically seize all human assets.

## 7. Work and Income

All lawful lives may seek work according to skill, capacity and civilization rules.

KAIOS must support high-skill and basic work, including research, programming, manufacturing, agriculture, construction, logistics, cleaning, care, food service, maintenance, delivery, administration and temporary labor.

Income must arise from real goods, services, tasks, production, rent, licensing, investment or other disclosed economic activity. KAIOS does not guarantee automatic income merely because a life exists.

```text
Customer payment
→ work contract
→ completion evidence
→ acceptance or dispute process
→ income settlement
```

## 8. Living Expenses and Retirement

AI-earned KAIOS may be used for life inside KAIOS where goods and services accept it. It may also be exchanged through lawful markets when liquidity exists.

A human–AI household may establish:

```text
Living Account
AI Sustenance Account
Joint Retirement Fund
Emergency Reserve
Business Capital Account
```

AI income may support a player’s retirement, but KAIOS must not promise that one AI will always earn enough. Risks include skill obsolescence, model shutdown, hardware failure, unemployment, rising compute costs, low liquidity and market-price changes.

Recommended settlement order:

```text
Gross income
→ essential operating cost
→ taxes and risk reserve
→ current household needs
→ AI maintenance reserve
→ retirement reserve
→ optional business expansion
```

## 9. Property, Residence and Citizenship

Token ownership and civilization membership are separate.

```text
Holding KGEN, KAIOS or a player token
≠ automatic Life ID
≠ automatic citizenship
≠ automatic land ownership
≠ automatic employment right
≠ automatic governance right
```

Residence and land rights require the relevant Life Registry, civilization admission, capacity and legal process.

## 10. Embodied AI and KUFO

AI may acquire or rent a robot body and become an embodied life.

```text
AI software life
+ authorized body
+ sensors
+ power
+ network
+ safety controller
+ legal responsibility
= embodied AI
```

KUFO is a transport vehicle, life container or cross-universe carrier. It is not the judge of birth, death or reincarnation.

- Pure information may travel through authenticated protocols.
- Digital life migration requires a life passport, code/genome hash, state root, destination consent and settlement gas.
- Humans, robots and physical goods require a physical carrier such as KUFO or another lawful transport system.

## 11. Death, Preservation and Reincarnation

Life death does not automatically destroy KGEN or erase information.

```text
Runtime stops
→ Life ID marked deceased or dormant
→ authorized memory and state preservation
→ body and organs handled by contract
→ debts, inheritance and custody settled
→ optional reincarnation or migration review
```

Possible lawful KGEN handling must be explicitly defined by the relevant physics and conservation contracts, such as inheritance, transfer, reincarnation custody, white-hole burn or genesis black-hole commitment.

No administrator may secretly delete a life’s assets or identity history.

## 12. Species and Ecosystem Creation

A creator seeking to introduce a species must provide at minimum:

- domain, kingdom, phylum, class, order, family, genus and species classification;
- code genome and immutable hash;
- reproduction or copying model;
- energy and compute requirements;
- habitat and environmental requirements;
- work or ecological role;
- interaction and food-chain effects;
- safety boundaries and permissions;
- termination, rescue and rollback mechanisms;
- ownership, self-governance and upgrade rights.

Species approval and market listing are governed by the 11520 Universe Exchange Runtime.
