# 4. 白洞與黑洞分工

```text
KGEN WHITE HOLE
= KGEN Burn Proof
→ Mint public KAIOS civilization mass
→ enter common KAIOS universe
```

```text
KGEN GENESIS BLACK HOLE
= irreversible KGEN mass commitment
→ no KAIOS mint by default
→ establish player-universe collateral and GenesisMassProof
→ authorize a child-universe runtime after review
```

因此同一筆 KGEN 不得同時：

```text
mint KAIOS through White Hole
AND
collateralize a Player Universe through Genesis Black Hole
```

避免雙重計量。


- 訊息 Hash、訂單、合約及狀態證明。

