## Three Realms

KAIOS uses the Three Realms as the civilization life-space grouping:

1. 地界 — underworld / lower-world life and judgment space.
2. 人界 — K280 Earth-surface human / biological reference world.
3. 天界 — higher civilization world; internally contains 仙界 and 神界 ranks.

Thus 神 and 仙 are subdivisions/ranks inside 天界, not additional replacements for the Three Realms.

## Higher time ladder

The same K280 Earth-surface duration may be represented by progressively shorter higher-level units:

1 K280 Earth-surface year
= 1 K18888 Heaven day
= 1 Chain-Boundary hour
= 1 Solar minute

This is a KAIOS civilization time-scale mapping. It does not alter BNB Chain block.timestamp or claim that real astrophysical clocks behave this way.
